Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Personal Assistant is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads, read-only, the owner's own Google account data: Gmail messages (bank alerts, statements, receipts), Contacts (names and birthdays) and Calendar events. It never sends, deletes, changes or marks anything as read.

Where data goes

Data is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to any third party, and not used for advertising. Short text excerpts may be processed by an AI model chosen by the owner, with account numbers masked first.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.
